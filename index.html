About

I am an experimental physicist working at the intersection of laboratory astrophysics, high-energy-density physics, and laser-plasma acceleration. My research uses controlled laboratory experiments to explore physical processes that also shape energetic astrophysical environments.

I received my M.Sc. in Astrophysics from Beijing Normal University and my Ph.D. in Physics from Osaka University. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at HZDR.
